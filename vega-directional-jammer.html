System Overview

Directional power for high-value protection.

VEGA is a multi-band directional electronic warfare system designed for long-range RF denial, counter-UAV operations, perimeter security, and strategic protection of high-value sites.

Instead of radiating equally in all directions, VEGA focuses jamming energy toward the threat direction. This provides stronger suppression effect, better control over the engagement sector, and an optimized response against UAVs approaching protected zones.
