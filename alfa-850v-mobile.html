SYSTEM OVERVIEW

ALFA 850V Mobile is a vehicle-mounted counter-UAV jammer system designed for mobile operations, convoys and tactical deployments. The system is housed in a ruggedized roof-mount enclosure and integrates directly onto tactical vehicles, SUVs and pickups.

It provides continuous counter-UAV protection across the 430 MHz to 6 GHz spectrum with 850 W total RF output, targeting UAV control links, GNSS, telemetry and video transmission simultaneously.
