WHO WE ARE

Mission-driven defense engineering

Alfasis Savunma Mühendislik A.Ş. is a Turkish defense technology company established in 2021, headquartered in Ankara. We develop and deliver advanced electronic warfare systems, tactical UAV platforms, and airborne EW solutions to defense and security organizations worldwide.

Our portfolio addresses three operational domains: RF suppression and electronic warfare, tactical and strategic drone systems, and the ALFAUAV airborne platform. Every system is engineered for real mission requirements.
