SYSTEM OVERVIEW

850 is a high-power, multi-band RF jammer designed to neutralize commercial and military-grade UAV threats. It delivers continuous and reliable jamming across multiple frequency bands, preventing command links, telemetry, and GNSS signals from reaching or leaving the UAV.

Its modular architecture enables flexible configuration, while the rugged design ensures long-term performance in the most demanding environments.
